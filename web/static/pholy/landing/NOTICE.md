Photographs are used only as illustrative marketing images. They are not
user uploads. Smaller variants are local derivatives. No external image
requests are made by visitors.

app-desktop.webp and app-mobile.webp show the actual PHOLY interface with
synthetic account data and the licensed example photographs above.
Reproduce the screenshots with web/test/medium/specs/pholy/landing-preview.spec.ts.
No production user data is used.

Instrument Serif and Inter are from https://github.com/google/fonts,
ofl/instrumentserif and ofl/inter. Their SIL Open Font License texts are
included alongside the fonts. Fonts are served locally.

Latin WOFF2 subsets are distributed by Fontsource via jsDelivr:
- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/inter:vf@latest/latin-wght-normal.woff2
- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/instrument-serif@latest/latin-400-normal.woff2
- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/instrument-serif@latest/latin-400-italic.woff2
